## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long do batteries typically last in budget wireless combos?**
Battery life varies wildly depending on the brand and your usage patterns. High-end budget options like the Logitech MK270 can last up to 36 months because they use highly efficient sleep modes. Lower-end options may require battery replacements every 3 to 6 months, so we recommend keeping a pack of alkaline batteries in your desk drawer.

**Is there a noticeable lag when using a budget wireless mouse and keyboard for office work?**
For standard office tasks like emailing, browsing, and spreadsheet management, you will not notice any lag. Most of these combos use 2.4GHz wireless technology via a USB dongle, which provides a near-instantaneous connection. However, if you are doing high-precision graphic design or gaming, you might notice a slight difference compared to a wired connection.

**Are these budget combos compatible with both Mac and Windows?**
Most budget wireless combos are designed with a "universal" layout that works across both operating systems. While the "Windows" key will act as the "Command" key on a Mac, the basic functionality remains the same. Always check the packaging for specific OS certifications, but generally, any USB-dongle-based set will work on any computer with a USB-A port.
